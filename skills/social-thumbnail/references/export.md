# Descriptive export procedure

Use the image editor/export interface you have. This skill does not bundle or create conversion scripts.

Keep the original high-resolution PNG and the editable typography/layout. Choose the real required canvas, file format, byte limit, crop/fit and encoder quality. Do not stretch proportions, overwrite the source or quietly change settings after a failed export.

A proportional crop removes part of the image. A contain operation keeps the whole source and adds a chosen background. Choose the treatment that keeps the content promise and all key text/subjects. Check every platform crop separately.

Apply orientation, and convert known color profiles properly for normal sRGB delivery. JPEG needs you to flatten alpha on purpose. An untagged image has no clear source color meaning, so check its real workflow instead of claiming the color is automatically correct.

Reopen the exported file. Check dimensions, real format, bytes, orientation, crop, glyphs and readability at small size. A quality setting of 99 does not promise a file size or that the image looks identical. If the chosen target fails, explain the specific tradeoff and use an approved adjustment.

Report the real master/copy dimensions, format, quality setting where available, bytes and check results. Do not claim you checked file properties or platform acceptance that you could not see.
