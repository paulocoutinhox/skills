# Social Thumbnail

Create a clear visual promise that the content actually fulfills. The image must remain understandable at the size and crop in which people encounter it. This skill explains design and export decisions without bundled converters, scripts, parser data or automatic updates.

## Brief and content evidence

Collect the actual content, audience, platform/surface, language, exact text, brand/style references, aspect ratio, pixel dimensions, upload device, required format and byte ceiling. Ask only unresolved consequential choices. Default to 16:9 when the surface is unspecified, while making the assumption visible.

Identify the moment, result, question or subject the content genuinely supports. A title can supply context while the thumbnail supplies visual evidence or curiosity. Do not imply an endorsement, fabricate a before/after, invent a feature or exaggerate a result the content never demonstrates.

Use [the brief template](assets/thumbnail-brief.md) to record the concept, focal subject, intended reading order, text, crop constraints, export target and evaluation question before generating artwork.

## Build hierarchy deliberately

Choose a dominant focal subject and a clear route through subject, headline and supporting detail. Group related elements by proximity, align them consistently, repeat a restrained visual vocabulary and establish meaningful contrast. Robin Williams's [The Non-Designer's Design Book, fourth edition](https://www.peachpit.com/store/non-designers-design-book-9780133966152) is professional further reading on those design principles, verified through the publisher record.

A short headline of roughly two to six words can be a useful starting point, but language and purpose control the final choice. If the viewer needs a paragraph to understand the image, revise the concept before shrinking the text.

Select type for actual display size and the content's tone. Avoid thin/ornate/condensed faces when they become illegible. Check diacritics, names, symbols, line breaks and letter spacing. Use a clear separation treatment where text would otherwise disappear into a busy background. Decorative shadows and outlines should clarify, not multiply clutter.

Use theme-appropriate color and a distinguishable focal point. Check grayscale to discover hierarchy that relies only on hue. Extreme saturation, red arrows or exaggerated faces are genre choices, not universal marketing requirements. [Practical Typography](https://practicaltypography.com/summary-of-key-rules.html) is useful further reading on type decisions, but its print-body recommendations must not be copied literally into thumbnail sizing.

## Compose for the actual placement

Work with the whole frame, then inspect likely feed/grid/player crops and interface overlays. Keep essential text/faces/results away from cutoffs and duration badges. A generic five-percent inset can start a composition, but cannot substitute for the actual surface's crop.

A square, landscape and portrait design need different arrangements. Do not squeeze a landscape subject or line of type into 9:16. Recompose, crop deliberately or extend supporting background. Preserve heads, hands, products, screens, circles and typography in correct proportion.

Recognition should not depend on recalling an unexplained symbol. The [Nielsen Norman Group discussion of recognition and recall](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/recognition-and-recall/) supplies relevant usability reasoning. Its interface findings are not a quantified promise of thumbnail CTR.

## Create and inspect imagery

Use the available image model for requested original artwork or image edits. A generated raster master is useful for illustration, while exact logos and brand typography may require editable vector/text composition. Preserve rights and actual source provenance.

Inspect generated anatomy, geometry, perspective, object count, lettering, face identity where relevant, shadows and physical relationships. Do not call a result professional merely because it is high resolution. Repair obvious defects before producing variants.

Use real product captures when demonstrating software. Decorative AI artwork must not become fabricated UI evidence. Add exact text with a suitable editable typography tool when the model's glyphs or alignment cannot be trusted.

## Platform and export profile

Research baseline: 2026-11-05. Stored limits are maintained only when the user requests an update. Check the actual target/interface when producing a deliverable, and state uncertainty without silently rewriting this skill.

| Surface | Researched canvas guidance | Important boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| YouTube video | Current official recommendation 3840×2160, 16:9, minimum width 640 | Current help distinguishes 2 MB mobile and 50 MB desktop video uploads |
| YouTube Shorts | Vertical 9:16, current guidance 2160×3840 | Custom cover/upload support depends on actual interface/account |
| Podcast playlist | Square 1:1 | Separate asset and byte requirements |
| Instagram/TikTok vertical cover | A 1080×1920 composition is a useful starting canvas | Grid/player/feed crops and native selection support differ |
| X/LinkedIn media | Actual post/video/link-preview placement | These are distinct products with distinct constraints |

The owner's portable preset is a large PNG master and a 16:9 JPEG derivative starting at quality 99 under 2,000,000 bytes. This is a conservative selected delivery profile, not a universal platform rule. Quality 99 is an encoder setting, not “99 percent identical,” and does not guarantee a 4K image fits.

Use the available editor/export interface to choose dimensions, fit/crop, quality and format explicitly. Never silently lower quality, crop away evidence, stretch artwork or replace the master after a size failure. If a selected export exceeds the ceiling, explain the actual tradeoff and use an already authorized adjustment or ask for the missing decision.

Apply actual orientation metadata. Convert known color profiles appropriately for typical sRGB delivery rather than stripping metadata while leaving incompatible pixel values. Flatten transparency onto a deliberate background for JPEG. Do not upscale a small source and claim it has new captured detail. See [the export procedure](references/export.md) and [platform detail](references/platforms.md).

## Worked storyboard and critique

For an actual tutorial about organizing reading notes, one concept can show a genuine scattered-versus-organized result with a short benefit headline. A second can focus on the successful search result and a more specific question. Both must reflect demonstrated content.

Review whether the difference is visible at approximately 320px wide. If both sides collapse into unreadable tiny cards, enlarge the actual evidence, reduce supporting objects or choose another concept. Enlarging a headline alone will not rescue an ambiguous visual story.

Inspect the actual exported file at full size and storefront/feed size. Check every glyph, subject proportion, crop, overlap, alignment and UI overlay. Reopen it and verify real format, dimensions and byte size through available file/editor properties. A filename extension does not prove encoding.

## Evaluate variants and deliver

When variants are requested, change a meaningful concept such as subject framing or demonstrated benefit. Do not present three nearly identical colors as independent creative hypotheses. Define the expected audience response and relevant metric before testing.

Current eligible YouTube tools can test up to three title/thumbnail combinations and evaluate watch-time performance. Verify actual availability. Pair click-through with retention/satisfaction and compare equivalent audiences. Sequential uploads at different times do not establish causal improvement.

Deliver the master, requested derivatives, editable text/layout where available, brief and actual export/inspection results. Preparing an image does not authorize publication or platform experiments. [Official thumbnail guidance](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72431?hl=en), [creator packaging guidance](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12340300?hl=en) and [source notes](references/sources.md) distinguish platform rules from these editorial recommendations.
