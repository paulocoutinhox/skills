## Reproducible findings

For each finding, record:

1. A short, specific bug title, severity and a separate priority.
2. Exact artifact/device/configuration, account role and starting state.
3. The shortest ordered steps and input to reproduce it.
4. Expected behavior from the agreed contract and the behavior you saw.
5. How often it happens and the outcome of every diagnostic rerun.
6. Cleaned screenshots/video, relevant logs and approved evidence of saved state.
7. User impact, affected paths and the proven cause if known.
8. Fix check and evidence for the regression paths around it, when a fix was requested.
