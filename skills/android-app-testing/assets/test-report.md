## Reproducible findings

For each finding, record:

1. Short concrete defect title, severity and separate priority.
2. Exact artifact/device/configuration, account role and initial state.
3. Minimal ordered reproduction steps and input.
4. Expected behavior from the agreed contract and observed behavior.
5. Frequency and every diagnostic rerun's outcome.
6. Sanitized screenshots/video, relevant logs and authorized durable-state evidence.
7. User impact, affected paths and a demonstrated cause if known.
8. Fix verification and surrounding regression evidence when a fix was requested.
