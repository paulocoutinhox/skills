The current commercial-help page's public client data was retrieved and read after static page extraction omitted the answers. It specifies PDF/X-1a, CMYK, outlined text, crop marks and bleed, images at least 300 ppi, and a single cover spread including flaps. It also lists common trim sizes and requires digital-proof approval before production. Its separate template answers conflict: one describes a generator, another says no downloadable templates, and an older answer links examples. Resolve the actual job specification rather than promising a particular generator or downloadable template. The numeric 4–5 mm bleed remains historical guidance from a separate page whose live body could not be reliably fetched.

The current commercial production form exposes trim, cover finish, interior paper, interior color, cover paper, cover color, lamination, and page count. The publicly readable form does not establish every available stock or binding. Obtain those selections from the actual job interface or template.

Academic edition checks use [the university normalization index](https://sibpesquisa.ufes.br/normas.html) and [the library's current normalization guidance](https://biblioteca.ufes.br/normalizacao). They identify NBR 14724:2024, NBR 10520:2023, and NBR 6023:2025. Consult the full authorized standards and institutional guide for a compliance claim. Arial, 12.5 mm paragraph indentation, cover design, and optional pagination positions are selected conventions, not universal normative requirements.

No production provider is named in skill instructions or finished-book templates. The repository's research guide records how production evidence and access limitations were handled without embedding provider branding in publications.
