# Editorial styles and ABNT-based defaults

## Select the correct scope

Academic presentation and commercial book design are different contexts. The default is an ABNT-based editorial profile, with configurable book typography and a white interior. For strict academic compliance, consult the current standard and institution's guide. Do not label a custom small-trim book, a centered page number, or a decorative font “strict ABNT” merely because it uses author-date citations.

Verified edition baseline: NBR 14724:2024, corrected in 2025, for academic presentation. NBR 10520:2023 for citations. NBR 6023:2025 for references. Also check applicable NBR 6024 for section numbering, NBR 6027 for contents, NBR 6028 for abstracts, and NBR 6029 for books. Verify later amendments before claiming compliance. This guide summarizes working decisions and does not reproduce the standards.

## Default style sheet

| Element | ABNT-based editorial preset | Classification |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Workbook page | A4, 210 × 297 mm, portrait. | Owner default. |
| Body | Arial, 12 pt, black on white, 1.5 line spacing. | Arial is an owner choice. The specific family is not universally mandated by ABNT. |
| Recto margins | Top and inner/left 30 mm, bottom and outer/right 20 mm. | Academic baseline, subject to verified institutional scope. |
| Duplex margins | Mirror inner/outer margins on verso pages. Increase gutter if manufacturing needs more room. | Production must fit the chosen binding. |
| Paragraph | First-line indent 12.5 mm, 0 pt extra between ordinary paragraphs, consistent alignment. | Owner editorial choice, not a universal ABNT indent. |
| Heading 1 | 14 pt bold, explicit hierarchy, new chapter on a new page where selected. | Editorial choice. |
| Heading 2 and 3 | 12 pt bold and 12 pt regular, consistent spacing and numbering. | Editorial choice. |
| Short quotation | Up to three lines, inline with quotation marks and a precise citation. | Citation baseline. |
| Long quotation | More than three lines, block treatment without quotation marks, smaller type and single spacing. Use 40 mm left indent as the selected recommended preset. | Verify current standard and institutional requirements. |
| Notes and sources | 10 pt, single spacing, linked note markers, consistent sequence. | Smaller size selected by the editorial profile. |
| Figure | Label, number, and selected title above. Source or credit below. Keep as one logical block. | Caption/title and source are distinct. |
| Tables | Number and title above, source and necessary notes below, repeated headers across pages. | Follow the selected table standard and avoid unreadable scaling. |
| References | Consistent entry style, single spacing, left alignment, visible separation between entries. | Verify NBR 6023 edition and institution guide. |
| Interior background | White/unpainted, including blank pages. No full-page tinted fill. | Mandatory owner production rule. |

Do not flatten all text into a single body style. Define separate styles for headings, inline emphasis, strong emphasis, foreign terms, direct quotes, block quotes, captions, source notes, footnotes, numbered lists, bullets, tables, equations, code, bibliographic entries, and cross-references. Code and equations may require other suitable fonts. Keep their backgrounds unpainted in print output.

## Citations, footnotes, and bibliography

Default to author-date citations with a bibliography. Keep source notes or explanatory footnotes where useful. If the user chooses numeric citation notes, use that system consistently and confirm its bibliography requirements. Do not mix systems arbitrarily or repeat a full reference in every footnote.

Use author names with the capitalization required by the current citation style, rather than mechanically uppercasing all parenthetical names. Direct quotations require a page or other precise locator when available. A paraphrase must accurately reflect its source. Label translations, omissions, or emphasis according to the selected style. Handle corporate authors, missing authors, multiple authors, same-author/same-year works, indirect citations, and online media through the current standard rather than inventing fields.

The bibliography must match actual cited sources. Collect author or organization, title and subtitle, edition, publication place where applicable, publisher, year, volume/issue/pages, DOI or stable URL, and access date where applicable. Handle books, chapters, articles, web pages, reports, legislation, theses, datasets, software, and audiovisual material with their type-specific fields. Do not fabricate missing metadata. Prefer originals over secondary mentions.

An automated CSL or bibliography style may implement an older standard edition. Inspect its documented version and rendered examples before calling it compliant with NBR 6023:2025. A typesetting engine does not itself verify reference quality.

## Front matter and pagination

Select elements appropriate to the publication. Do not insert an academic approval sheet or abstract into every commercial book. Copyright information and an ISBN must be real. A professional cataloging record must not be invented by the model.

Academic mode counts pages according to the selected standard and displays numbers beginning at the appropriate textual section. Verify treatment of the title page, cataloging page, and duplex versos. Strict academic recto/verso placement can differ from the user's freely chosen book pagination. For editorial books, document the selected counting and display policy explicitly. Suppress running heads on selected opening or blank pages without breaking the count.
