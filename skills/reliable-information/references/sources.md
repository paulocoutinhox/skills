# Research-method sources

Verified on 2026-11-05. These sources govern specific discovery/status checks, not the truth of
every future answer. Each task still needs sources relevant to its actual topic.

- [Google Scholar search help](https://scholar.google.com/intl/en/scholar/help.html): discovery,
  title/author searches, citations, related articles, access, and coverage limitations.
- [Google Scholar overview](https://scholar.google.com/intl/en/scholar/about.html): heterogeneous
  scholarly materials, explaining why indexing alone is not a peer-review or quality guarantee.
- [Crossmark](https://www.crossref.org/services/crossmark/): publisher-reported corrections,
  retractions, and updates. Participation/metadata coverage limits the absence of notices.
- [Crossref Retraction Watch](https://www.crossref.org/documentation/retrieve-metadata/retraction-watch/):
  another status-check route, with different coverage for retractions and other notices.
- [COPE retraction guidelines](https://doi.org/10.24318/cope.2019.1.4): ethical status and linked
  publication notices. Apply the current version and preserve document licensing.

No book or inaccessible standard is claimed as fully read. Source count, publisher brand, citation
count, and the presence of a DOI do not independently certify a factual answer. No automated research
service or independent model benchmark was created or executed during authoring.
