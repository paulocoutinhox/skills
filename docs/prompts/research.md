# Research prompts

Answers backed by sources you can check. Read [how to use the prompts](../task-prompts.md) before

## Reliable information

```text
Use reliable-information from paulocoutinhox/skills.
Read [SKILLS_ROOT]/reliable-information/SKILL.md and name the path really loaded. If it is missing, say so.

Research [QUESTION] for [AUDIENCE_AND_DECISION], within [TIMEFRAME_JURISDICTION_OR_VERSION].
Use the sources that fit: primary sources, academic search such as Google Scholar, material from
qualified professionals, trusted books, and well-known news outlets. Look at the real evidence,
methods, scope, dates, corrections and retractions, and important disagreements. Cite the direct
source next to each claim. Mark what is inferred and what is uncertain. Do not suggest you read in
full a work you did not read.
If you cannot confirm reliable support, say so and hold back the unsupported conclusion or
advice. Do not make up facts, sources, credentials, citations, or agreement among experts.
```
